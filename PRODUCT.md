# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in Nigeria who hold Naira and want crypto in the same place: buy, sell, swap, send, and top up airtime or data without juggling apps. They reach this site to decide whether Blox is safe enough to hand over identity documents (KYC) and money.

Assumed, not yet confirmed by the owner: most arrive on a phone, often a mid-range Android on mobile data, from a link someone shared.

## Product Purpose

Blox is one account for Naira and crypto. Tagline: "Naira and crypto. One simple app."

What the app does:

- Account: sign up, KYC, a unique Blox ID per user, transaction PIN, biometrics, OTP/2FA.
- Wallet: Naira balance plus several crypto assets, with quick actions (Buy, Sell, Send, Receive, Swap, Airtime, Data).
- Send and receive: Blox to Blox by Blox ID, instant, with the recipient's verified name shown before you confirm. Also external crypto addresses, with QR and network warnings.
- Crypto: buy, sell and swap. Rate, fees and the amount you get are shown before you confirm. Deposits and withdrawals carry clear statuses.
- Bills: airtime and data for Nigerian networks.
- History and alerts: searchable transactions, notifications on every status change.

This repo is the marketing site: Home, Features, Security, Fees, Help, and Waitlist or Get the app. It holds no user data and builds to static HTML. Success is a visitor who understands what Blox does, trusts it, and goes to get the app.

## Brand Personality

Assumed from the tagline and feature set, not yet confirmed: plain, sturdy, bright. Short declarative sentences. Says what happens to your money and when.

Brand kit: v5, expected in `blox-brand-v5/` (not in this repo yet). Violet `#7B2CFF`, ink `#0E0B14`.

## Anti-references

Assumed, not yet confirmed:

- Crypto-exchange neon on black, glow, gradient text, glass panels.
- Trading-terminal density, or tickers showing rates the site cannot back with live data.
- Corporate bank navy and stock-photo handshakes.

## Design Principles

1. Show it before you confirm. The app shows rate, fee and recipient name before anything moves. The site is just as upfront: no claim without the detail next to it.
2. One simple app. One idea per screen, one main action in view.
3. The block is the brand. A single violet block is the recurring object: it marks where you are and moves the way value moves in the app.
4. Phone first, light on data. Everything works on a small screen and a slow connection before it gets richer on desktop.
5. Trust through clarity. Plain words and solid shapes do the reassuring, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Assumed default: WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard access, visible focus, touch targets of at least 44px, and a reduced-motion version of every animation.
